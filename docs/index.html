QuizMaster — Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

1. No account required. You can play QuizMaster without creating an account. The app itself does not collect your email, name, or any personal identifier.

2. Data stays on your device. The following are stored only in your browser's or app's local storage on your own device:

  • Your chosen subject, difficulty, and AI-provider preference
  • Your OpenRouter API key — only if you choose to enter it
  • The last two generated questions (question history), plus short local dedup memory used to avoid repeating questions
  • Cached reference-source results and on-device model files, used to make generation faster and available offline

This data never leaves your device and is never uploaded, sold, or shared.

3. Optional AI providers. QuizMaster can use on-device AI or optional cloud AI providers:

  • WebLLM runs entirely on your device — prompts never leave your device. The app bundles on-device model weights (Qwen3.5-0.8B and Llama-3.2-3B) inside the app package (Android APK distributed via Aptoide), so no model download or server connection is required for on-device generation.
  • Puter and OpenRouter are third-party cloud services you opt into by signing in or entering an API key. Your prompts and answers are sent to those providers; review their privacy policies before use.

QuizMaster is not responsible for third-party providers' privacy practices.

4. Live sources. Questions are grounded in live web sources (for example Wikipedia) fetched at quiz time. No personal data is sent with those fetches.

5. Children. QuizMaster does not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13.

6. Your rights. You can clear all stored data by removing the app or clearing your browser's site data for this origin.

7. Contact. Questions about this policy — contact the app developer.

QuizMaster — App Information

Last updated: November 2026

QuizMaster turns any subject into a live, source-verified trivia game. It ships as one app with two fully independent modules, each with its own design system, AI providers, and device-only storage:

SageStrike — A tactical campaign quiz (Campaign, Blitz and Gauntlet modes) grounded in live web sources, with AI hints, a persistent skill tree, and an unlimited custom mode that keeps generating fresh, non-repeating questions one at a time (no shield-based game over).

BrainForge — An on-device quiz arena powered by WebLLM that runs AI locally on your device, with optional Puter sign-in and OpenRouter API-key support. Fully offline-first and private.

Built-in on-device AI. The app ships with bundled WebLLM models (Qwen3.5-0.8B and Llama-3.2-3B) inside the app package, so quizzes can be generated with no runtime download and no backend — every answer is produced on your device. A dual-model relay pipeline can alternate a fast model and a stronger model across questions for speed and quality.

Questions are verified against cited source passages so you answer from real material rather than a model's memory. Your question history is kept only on your device (capped at the two most recent entries) and is never uploaded or sold.
